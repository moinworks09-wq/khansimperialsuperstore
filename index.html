Our Story

Four Decades of
Trust & Tradition.

What started in 1984 as a small family-run halal butcher in Woking has grown into something the whole community calls their own. Khans Imperial SuperStore has served generation after generation — and we still know most of our customers by name.

Every cut of meat is prepared in-house by butchers who have spent decades at the block. Our fruit and vegetables arrive fresh every morning — never sitting on a shelf longer than they need to.

We believe a great grocer should feel like a neighbour. Somewhere you can ask questions, trust what you're buying, and take home something your family will love.

Our Journey
From a Single Stall to an Imperial Store
1984
The Beginning

Khans opens its doors as a small halal butcher in Woking, serving the local community with fresh, affordable, trusted cuts. From day one the commitment was simple: quality you can count on.

1990s
Growing the Community

Word spread quickly. Khans became the go-to halal butcher for families across Woking and surrounding areas — fresh lamb, chicken and beef prepared in-house every single morning.

2000s
Expanding the Range

Fresh fruit and vegetables were added to the range, making Khans a true one-stop shop for the whole family. The SuperStore name was earned through years of consistent quality.

Today
Khans Imperial SuperStore

Four decades on, we're still family-run, still cutting fresh every morning, and still serving Woking the way we always have. Now taking orders online too — same care, even faster.
